Questions

Good to know

Do you deliver in Patna?

Yes, across Patna city for orders above ₹500. Most orders reach you the same day.

How long do the sweets stay fresh?

Milk sweets are best in 2 days, kept cool. Laddu, khaja and thekua stay good for about a week.

Can I order for a wedding or puja?

Of course. Send the date and quantity on WhatsApp and we will share a quote the same day.

Do you have sugar free sweets?

Our dry fruit laddu has no added sugar. Ask at the counter for the day's sugar free barfi.
